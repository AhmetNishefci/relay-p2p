# AI assistance disclosure

## Development approach

Relay was developed through a candidate-directed workflow using Codex. The candidate
selected and approved the product concept, principal design decisions, milestone
scope and priorities, and authorized successive implementation phases.

Beyond the retained upstream template, the application implementation, tests,
artwork and assessment documentation were generated by AI. The recorded history
does not document candidate-authored implementation or completed independent human
code review. Product direction and milestone approvals are distinguished from code
authorship; Git author identity is not evidence of manual implementation.

## Scope of AI assistance

AI assistance covered source research, architecture proposals, implementation,
debugging, test drivers, Android verification, visual QA and documentation.

Existing upstream code remains credited to Holepunch. The worker began as the
assessment-required copy of `hello-pear-worker`, extended with AI-generated
application integration. Original license and notices are retained.

## Engineering decisions and development process

The candidate approved Relay's explicit acknowledgment handoff and the two principal
assessment decisions: Sync state and Pairing/trust. Codex proposed implementation
details, generated the changes and ran the recorded checks under those instructions.
The rationale, alternatives and trade-offs are documented in the
[README decisions](../README.md#decisions).

Development proceeded through scoped conventional commits, focused tests and real
Android peer verification. Phase reports distinguish executed results, failed
attempts, corrections and untested behavior.

## Authentic AI records and provenance

The [genuine visible transcript export](ai/codex-visible.jsonl.gz) contains recorded
user/assistant messages and tool calls/results. Its [reading instructions](ai/README.md)
and [integrity manifest](ai/manifest.json) describe selection, privacy redactions,
source/export hashes, record counts and the exact capture cutoff. It is a filtered,
privacy-redacted export, not an unmodified original or a reconstructed summary.

The known Codex session is `01a18158-3cb1-7e70-a804-2b4d3c33e634`, with original filename
`rollout-2026-10-28T08-10-06-01a18158-3cb1-7e70-a804-2b4d3c33e634.jsonl`.
The unmodified original remains in the private Codex session store; byte snapshots
and hashes are retained under ignored `.assessment-local/ai/`. Private originals
can be compared against manifest source hashes but are not accessible through the
repository. Internal approval-review sessions are not candidate conversations.

Internal instructions/reasoning and image data are excluded. Possible capability
strings, credentials and unrelated machine/home identifiers receive declared masks;
message text is not summarized. Transcript refreshes preserve all previously exported
records, including historical wording in genuine messages.

## Verification and limitations

Technical results and their platform limits are recorded in the
[final readiness audit](PHASE9.md) and individual phase reports.

The assessment requires AI records and excludes fully AI-coded submissions. The
recorded development approach above does not establish eligibility under that policy;
this disclosure does not claim that the restriction has been satisfied.

The publication export includes the documentation review and publication authorization
through its manifest cutoff. Later publication steps and the final report remain in
the ongoing original until a subsequent refresh. Each refresh requires review of
privacy replacements and verification of hashes. Any additional AI tools or sessions
require candidate confirmation and inclusion; repository evidence alone cannot establish
that no other assistance occurred.
